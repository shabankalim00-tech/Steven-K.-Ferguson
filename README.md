# Steven K. Ferguson LLC — Agricultural Consulting

Strategic consulting for agriculture and rural enterprise — helping you grow what matters, sustainably.

## 🌿 About
For over two decades, Steven K. Ferguson has helped farms, cooperatives, and rural businesses navigate complexity with clarity. We deliver strategies built on deep industry knowledge and a genuine commitment to your legacy.

## 🛠️ Core Services
- **Agricultural Consulting:** Crop & land strategy, soil health programs, sustainability planning.
- **Business Advisory:** Financial structuring, operational audits, growth planning.
- **Farm Operations & Management:** Workflow optimization, equipment & resource management, field-level efficiency.
- **Strategic Planning:** Multi-year roadmaps, risk management, market analysis.
- **Compliance & Risk Support:** Regulatory guidance, environmental standards, liability planning.
- **Community & Economic Development:** Cooperative support, rural development, regional strategy.
